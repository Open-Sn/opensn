class CellFaceOrientations

The orientation of every face of every local cell with respect to a sweep direction.

Every direction of an unstructured mesh has its own orientations, so they are stored flat: the faces of cell c are entries offsets[c] to offsets[c + 1] - 1, in the order of the cell’s faces.
